The solution

Five contracts, deployed and source-verified on Arc testnet

1
RialtoOracle
Pull-based FX feed. Publishers sign EIP-712 attestations; the caller names its own staleness bound; a circuit breaker caps any print at 10% fresh, 25% ever.
2
RialtoPool
Stableswap on rate-scaled balances — the cheap part of the curve sits on the real rate and moves with it. 25bp, half to LPs, half to the protocol, in code.
3
RialtoSettlement
Exact-output invoice settlement: denominated in what the payee receives, paid to a third party, reference and rate emitted on-chain.
4
RialtoRouter
Multi-hop exact-output: N corridors give N(N−1)/2 pairs.
5
RialtoForward
Collateralised, cash-settled rate locks for invoices with terms.

Scope · said plainly

Which part of the payment this is

Getting in — not us

Circle Mint, an exchange, or a USDC balance the business already holds.

The FX leg — this

USDC to the exact euro amount billed, at the real rate, 25bp, one transaction, invoice reference on-chain. Nothing on Arc could do this.

Getting out — not us

The payee holds EURC, or cashes to a local account through a partner — several are Circle grantees.

No KYC, no fiat ramps, no local payout, no licence to hold client money. Circle already funds that network; what it cannot do today is convert at a rate the chain can verify.
